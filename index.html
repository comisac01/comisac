About Conference

ComSIA 2027 is organised with the objective of bringing together innovative scientists, professors, research scholars, students and industrial experts in the field of Sciences, Computational Methods and Techniques to a common platform. The primary goal of the Symposium is to promote the exchange of innovative scientific information between researchers, developers, engineers, students, and practitioners. Overall the Conference will provide the researchers and attendees with prospects for national and international collaboration and networking among universities and institutions from India and abroad for promoting research and development. ComSIA 2027 will be jointly organized by School of Open Learning, University of Delhi and Shaheed Rajguru College of Applied Sciences, University of Delhi and Silicon University, Odisha on 5-6th March, 2027. All the accepted papers (after double blinded peer review) will be published in Springer Lecture Notes on Networks & Systems (Indexing: SCOPUS, INSPEC, WTI Frankfurt eG, zbMATH, SCImago; All books published in the series are submitted for consideration in Web of Science) and extended selected papers will be published in the special issues of SCI/SCOPUS/WoS/DBLP/ACM indexed Journals. The associated journal to consider the high-quality extended papers:

1. Alexandria Engineering Journal, Elsevier, SCI
2. Intelligent Decision Technologies, IOS Press, SCOPUS, ESCI
3. International Journal of Cognitive Computing in Engineering (IJCCE), ESCI, EI, SCOPUS
